This policy covers refunds for paid deep reports purchased on Tarot Tools. Deep reports are digital goods delivered by link, so our policy is built around whether the report was generated and whether you have opened it. All readings and reports are for reflection and entertainment only; dissatisfaction with the content of a random draw is not by itself a basis for refund. The cases below are.

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

## 1. Report not generated: automatic refund

If your report has not been generated within **10 minutes** of successful payment, the order is automatically refunded in full to your original payment method. You do not need to contact us. Stripe typically returns the funds within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. If you believe a refund should have been triggered but has not appeared, email us with your order token.

## 2. Report generated but not opened: full refund within 7 days

If the report was generated but you have **not opened or downloaded** it, you can request a full refund within **7 days** of purchase. Email us with your order token. Once we confirm the report is unopened, we will refund in full and revoke the report link.

"Opened" means the report link was accessed or the file was downloaded.

## 3. Report opened: case by case

If you have opened the report, the digital good has been delivered, and a refund is not automatic. We will still consider a request if, for example, the report is clearly broken, truncated, or does not match the cards you drew; it was opened by mistake and you contacted us promptly; or a technical fault on our side affected your purchase.

Email us within 14 days of purchase with your order token and a short description. We will reply with a decision: a full refund, a partial refund, or a regenerated report.

## 4. Duplicate charges

If you were charged more than once for the same report, email us and we will refund the duplicate in full.

## 5. How to request a refund

Send an email to **support@tarot-tools.example.com** with:

- Your **order token** (shown on the confirmation page and in the delivery email).
- The email address used at checkout, if any.
- A brief reason for the request.

We aim to respond within a few business days. Refunds go to the original payment method through Stripe.

## 6. What is not covered

Disagreement with the content of a random card draw, and requests made more than 14 days after purchase (except duplicate charges). Purchases by anyone under 18 are not permitted under our [terms](/guides/terms); contact us and we will handle these individually.
