Please read this before using any reading, card meaning, spread guide, or report on this site.

## Entertainment and self-reflection only

Everything on Tarot Tools is provided **for reflection and entertainment only**. Tarot cards are drawn at random. The interpretations we attach to them are editorial writing intended to prompt thought, journaling, and conversation. They are not predictions, and they do not reveal hidden facts about you, other people, or the future.

## Not professional advice

Nothing on this site is, or should be treated as:

- **Medical advice.** If you have a health concern, speak to a doctor or other qualified health professional.
- **Legal advice.** For legal questions, consult a qualified lawyer.
- **Financial advice.** For decisions about money, investments, debt, or tax, consult a qualified financial professional. A money reading is a reflection on your attitudes, not a recommendation.
- **Psychological or mental health advice.** Tarot can be a useful journaling tool, but it is not therapy. If you are struggling, please reach out to a mental health professional or a local support line.

If you are in crisis or in danger, contact your local emergency services immediately.

## No claims of accuracy

We make no claims that readings are accurate, that they predict events, or that they reflect anything beyond the random draw and the editorial text. Two people drawing the same card will read it differently, and that is as it should be. You remain fully responsible for any decision you make, and you agree not to rely on this site as the basis for decisions with real consequences.

## AI-assisted content

Free readings are produced by a deterministic rules engine written by our editorial team and do not involve AI. Optional paid deep reports are generated with the help of a language model and are **clearly labelled as AI-assisted**. AI text can be fluent and still wrong, generic, or inconsistent. Treat it as an extended prompt for reflection, not as an authority. AI never changes the cards you drew. See [how our generator works](/methodology/) for details.
